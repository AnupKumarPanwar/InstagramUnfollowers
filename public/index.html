Instagram Automation
Runs in this browser

Two jobs. One script.

Follow and unfollow, from the tab you already have open.

This is an Instagram automation tool. It reviews people who do not follow you back, and it can follow another account’s followers page by page. Nothing is installed.

01

Non-followers

Scan who does not follow you back, protect a whitelist, and unfollow only the accounts you select.

02

Follow their followers

Enter a username. Each page of followers is followed as it arrives, with the same delays and safety stops.

Get the script Copy, then paste
  1. Copy the script.
  2. Open instagram.com and sign in.
  3. Paste it in the browser console and press Enter.

Or drag this bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar, open Instagram, and click it.
